The one rule that keeps you alive

Risk ≤ 1% of margin, always

  • Margin RM8,000 → max loss per trade = RM80
  • RM80 ÷ RM25 per point = 3.2 points of stop distance
  • So your stop-loss must sit ≤ ~3 points from entry
  • Three losses in a row still leaves 97% of your capital
  • A 10-point stop = RM250 = 3.1% → three losses = −9.4%. That's how accounts die.

Module 3 · Trend tools

Moving averages steer, ADX tells you if there's an engine

SMA smooths price equally; EMA reacts faster. A fast EMA crossing above a slow EMA = bullish crossover; below = bearish crossover (see chart in Module 2). But a crossover in a dead market is a trap — that's what ADX is for.

Direction, not strength

+DI vs −DI (the DMI lines)

  • +DI above −DI → buyers dominate → look for longs only
  • −DI above +DI → sellers dominate → look for shorts only
  • A fresh crossover is the entry trigger (circled on the slide charts)
  • ADX is the referee: it says whether the crossover deserves your money

On the Module 2 chart, green line = +DI, red line = −DI, navy = ADX. Circles mark the crossovers.

The enemy

Whipsaws, explained simply

A whipsaw is a door that opens just enough to trap you, then slams shut. Price pokes through support, triggers your entry or stop, and reverses.

  • Wide-enough stop = 1.5 × ATR (course rule; cuts false stops by 92.4% — Rakuten RS-FCPO-2025-087)
  • But never wider than your 1% money rule — if those clash, skip the trade

The six-gate checklist (copy to your notes)

FCPO ENTRY CHECKLIST — all gates must pass
1. TREND   : ADX ≥ 18 and rising
2. DIRECTION: fresh +DI/−DI cross in trade direction
3. CANDLES : ≥3 consecutive Heikin Ashi candles in direction (or engulfing/hammer)
4. VOLUME  : ≥ +20% vs 7-day average
5. CALENDAR: not Oct–Dec; no MPOB/news release within 1 hour
6. RISK    : stop ≤ 1% of margin (RM80 @ RM8k) → ≤ ~3 pts @ RM25/pt; target ≥ 2× risk
